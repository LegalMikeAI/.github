## LegalMike

**Making the law accessible.**

[LegalMike](https://legalmike.ai) is legal research for Dutch and EU law, built
for lawyers and legal professionals. It searches primary sources — case law,
legislation, disciplinary law, local regulations, EU law, ECHR case law,
dispute committees, parliamentary papers and legal literature — and links every
ruling and article it relies on, so each answer can be verified at the source.
